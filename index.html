About Me

Hello! I'm Anderson Hilliard, an English major at Howard University and a member of the Humanities and Social Sciences Scholars Program. Originally from Atlanta, Georgia, I'm passionate about literature, film, creative writing, and the humanities, particularly the ways language, storytelling, and art shape how we understand ourselves and the world around us. My academic interests include African American literature and history, cultural studies, film studies, and the intersections of identity and artistic expression. Outside the classroom, I'm a writer, musician, and lifelong lover of the arts. I enjoy writing poetry, short stories, and screenplays, playing guitar, exploring different forms of creative expression, and engaging with the world through reading and film. Ultimately, I hope to pursue a PhD in English Literature, continue developing my voice as a writer and artist, and build a life centered around creativity, scholarship, and meaningful contributions to the world.
